Privacy Policy – Color Mixer

Effective Date:24 October 2026

🎨 About Color Mixer

Color Mixer is a creative tool that helps users mix colors through two professional blending systems:

  • Vivid / Natural (RYB): Painter-style color mixing
  • Classic / Digital (RGB): Display-based color blending
  • 8-slot system with adjustable quantities
  • 90+ color variations per base color
  • Favorites and palette management features

1. Data Collection

Stored locally (on your device only):

  • Color recipes, palettes, favorites
  • Mixing method & UI preferences

Collected by third-party services (ads or analytics):

If the app uses third-party advertising or analytics services, those services may automatically collect limited, non-personal device information such as:

  • Advertising ID (anonymous identifier)
  • Approximate location (region-level)
  • Device type, model, and OS version
  • App usage and performance data

We use the Android Advertising ID to serve ads and analyze app usage. This identifier is used only for advertising and analytics purposes. It is not linked to personally identifiable information such as your name or email.

We do not link your Advertising ID with your Google account information (such as name or email). These data sources are handled separately and are not combined for profiling.

We do not collect or store personal information such as name or email on our own servers. Starting from the latest release, login functionality has been removed. For users on earlier app versions, authentication and account data were securely processed via Firebase Authentication.

The app may use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to collect anonymous usage data such as app interactions, screen views, and feature usage. This data does not include personally identifiable information and is used only to improve app performance and user experience.

The app may use Firebase Remote Config (a Google service) to dynamically adjust app features, behavior, and update settings without requiring a new app version. This may involve collecting non-personal technical information such as app version, device type, and usage context. This data is used only to improve functionality and user experience and does not include personally identifiable information.

2. User Login (Google Sign-In Notice)

Important Note: Starting from the latest update, the Google Sign-In / Login feature has been completely removed from Color Mixer. Users on the latest version are not required to log in or provide any personal account information.

For users running older versions of the app who previously signed in, authentication was securely handled by Google Firebase Authentication, accessing only public profile details (such as display name and email address) to personalize in-app features.

If you signed in on an older version and wish to request deletion of any previously stored account records or app data, please refer to Section 8 (Data Deletion Request) or contact us directly.

3. Purpose of Use

  • Provide and improve color-mixing features
  • Remember user settings and preferences
  • We may show personalized or non-personalized advertisements using third-party services such as Google AdMob. Users can control ad personalization through their device settings.
  • Analyze performance and improve stability

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personally identifiable information.

Non-personal data may be shared only with advertising or analytics services for delivering, measuring, or improving app performance.

5. Your Choices & Control

  • Switch between RYB and RGB modes anytime in-app
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Device Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Clear all local data anytime: Settings → Apps → Color Mixer → Storage → Clear Data

For users in regions where consent is required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and advertising consent choices. The availability and type of consent options may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

Where applicable, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices through the privacy options provided in the App. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is shown.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us to remove it.

7. Security

All data in Color Mixer is stored locally on your device. Where network services or legacy authentication features were utilized, data was securely processed through Google Firebase services over encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Data Deletion & Account Removal Request

Since login is removed in the latest app version, all color data, palettes, and settings are stored strictly on your local device. You can permanently erase this data at any time via Device Settings → Apps → Color Mixer → Storage → Clear Data.

If you previously created an account on an older version of the app and wish to request the deletion of any associated account records or data, please send an email request to rdroidapps096@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - Color Mixer". We will process and delete your data promptly.
